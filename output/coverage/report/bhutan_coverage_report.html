How the numbers are counted

Recorded day
A local calendar day with a daily value from the sensor. With ≥75% of hours selected, the day also needs OpenAQ's coverage_percent of at least 75, meaning 18 or more of 24 hourly readings.
Active span
The days from a sensor's first to its last recorded day. For a single year it is clipped to that year, so a sensor installed in October has a span of about 90 days that year, not 365.
Missing days
Days inside the active span with no recorded day. Days before a sensor's first report or after its last report don't count as missing.
Outage
A run of consecutive missing days longer than the selected threshold. The line chart and the longest-outages list work per location: days are merged across every sensor at the station, so a station that comes back under a new OpenAQ sensor id still shows the break. A location that stopped for good is not counted after its last report. The sensor timelines in the map panel show the same gaps for each sensor.
Station total
The first block in the map panel merges every sensor at the location for the selected pollutant: a day counts once if any of them recorded it, and the span runs from the station's first report to its last. The map colors use the same count. This is the row to read for stations OpenAQ moved to a new sensor id after a break.
